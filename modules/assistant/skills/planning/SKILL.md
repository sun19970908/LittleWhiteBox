# Plans and independent subtasks

## When does a checklist help?

A checklist keeps the steps and their outcomes in the current conversation. Use it for work with several stages, a long investigation, or work likely to be resumed later. Record concrete outcomes and update them as the work progresses. Existing items and their details are available through the plan tools.

## What can be delegated?

A useful subtask has a clear scope and a result the main assistant can incorporate. Include the needed paths, facts, constraints and expected result; the delegate receives that brief rather than the parent conversation. Independent investigation can be delegated while the main conversation retains decisions about the overall work and the final reply.

Delegates can read skills to enable the tools their task needs. Plans and further delegation belong to the main conversation.
